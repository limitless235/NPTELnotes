# References — Information Security

## Primary textbooks

1. **Stallings, W.** (2017). *Cryptography and Network Security: Principles and Practice* (7th ed.). Pearson.  
   Comprehensive coverage of symmetric/asymmetric crypto, network security, and system security.

2. **Kahate, A.** (2013). *Cryptography and Network Security* (3rd ed.). Tata McGraw-Hill.  
   Widely used in Indian universities; accessible treatment of classical and modern cryptography.

3. **Stallings, W., & Brown, L.** (2018). *Computer Security: Principles and Practice* (4th ed.). Pearson.  
   Broader security topics: OS security, trusted computing, auditing.

4. **Pfleeger, C. P., Pfleeger, S. L., & Margulies, J.** (2015). *Security in Computing* (5th ed.). Pearson.  
   Security threats, program security, and policy frameworks.

## Cryptography references

5. **Menezes, A. J., van Oorschot, P. C., & Vanstone, S. A.** (1996). *Handbook of Applied Cryptography*. CRC Press.  
   [https://cacr.uwaterloo.ca/hac/](https://cacr.uwaterloo.ca/hac/) — free online reference.

6. **Katz, J., & Lindell, Y.** (2020). *Introduction to Modern Cryptography* (3rd ed.). CRC Press.  
   Rigorous theoretical foundations with provable security.

7. **Schneier, B.** (1996). *Applied Cryptography* (2nd ed.). Wiley.  
   Classic practical reference for algorithms and protocols.
