## Topic clusters

### Block A — Contingency planning (L11–L12)

- Incident response phases and IRT roles
- Forensics, severity tiers, regulatory notification
- DR vs. BCP; RTO, RPO, MTPD
- Backup strategies, alternate sites, testing

### Block B — Cybersecurity policy (L13–L15)

- EISP/ESSP structure and purpose
- ISSP domains (AUP, email, remote access, BYOD)
- SySSP for specific systems
- Policy lifecycle, culture, awareness, HR/legal integration

### Block C — Risk management (L16–L18)

- Threat, vulnerability, asset, impact
- Qualitative vs. quantitative (ALE, SLE, ARO)
- Treatment: mitigate, transfer, avoid, accept
- Third-party risk, KRIs, board reporting

### Block D — Industry perspective (L19–L20)

- Threat landscape and attack categories
- Defense technologies overview
- Kill chain / ATT&CK
- Exploits, threat intelligence, sector-specific risks

## Policy hierarchy reference

```
EISP (Enterprise)
  ├── ISSP (Issue-specific: AUP, email, BYOD, …)
  └── SySSP (System-specific: ERP, HR, SCADA, …)
        └── Standards → Procedures
```

- [ ] IR phases and IRT roles; containment vs. eradication

- [ ] Kill chain stages; top exploit enablers
