## Topic clusters

### Block A — Motivation and scope (L1–L3)

- Why managers need cybersecurity and privacy literacy
- Real incidents: phishing, ransomware, IoT, critical infrastructure
- Information security vs. cybersecurity; course roadmap

### Block B — CIA foundations (L4–L6)

- Confidentiality, integrity, availability triad
- Target breach case study
- Security domains, governance preview, ISO/NIST introduction

### Block C — GRC (L7–L9)

- Governance, risk, compliance integration
- Policy hierarchy; ISO 27001/27002; NIST CSF
- Metrics, risk registers, ERM alignment

### Block D — Contingency start (L10)

- IR, DR, BCP distinctions
- NIST incident response lifecycle
- Relationship to risk management and policy
